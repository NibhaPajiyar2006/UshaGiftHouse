Our policies.

Shipping Policy

Delivery charges and estimated delivery dates are confirmed when your order is accepted. Delivery times can vary by destination, courier availability, holidays and busy seasons. Please provide a complete delivery address and a working phone number, and contact us with your order details if your parcel is delayed.

Return and Refund Policy

If an order arrives damaged, incomplete or different from what was confirmed, contact us as soon as possible at ushagifthouse01@gmail.com with your order details and clear photos. We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate resolution in line with applicable consumer law. As some hampers contain food or are customised, change-of-mind returns may not be accepted once an order has been prepared.

Privacy Policy

We use details you provide, such as your name, email, phone number and order information, to respond to enquiries, manage orders and provide requested updates. Newsletter subscriber names may appear publicly on our Our Subscribers page; please use a name you are comfortable displaying. Subscriber and review submissions are stored using Google services. We do not sell personal information. To request an update or removal, or to stop newsletter messages, contact us at ushagifthouse01@gmail.com.

Terms of Service

Product photos are illustrative; colours, packaging and availability may vary. Prices, contents, customisation, delivery charges and timelines are confirmed with you before an order is accepted. An order is confirmed only after Usha Gift House has agreed the order details with you. Please check your order and delivery details carefully before confirming. We may update product information and these terms when needed; applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.
